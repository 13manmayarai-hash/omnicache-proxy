Four checks.
Under a millisecond.

  1. 01

    Intercept

    Sits on localhost between your agent and the API.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Git SHA and mtime checks. Edited files are never replayed.

  3. 03

    Replay

    Exact repeats are served from memory.

  4. 04

    Match

    Reworded prompts match too, with stricter rules for code.

Miss? Forwarded once, then cached.
